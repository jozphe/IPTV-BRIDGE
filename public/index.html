The method

Three moving parts, one clean result inside your player.
01

Connect

Drop in Xtream credentials or an M3U URL. We parse channels, movies and series in one pass and cache them warm.

02

Resolve

Titles are cleaned of release tags and matched to TMDB — posters, years, ratings and IMDB/TMDB IDs all line up.

03

Stream

Search anything in Stremio or Nuvio and your own IPTV source appears as a playable stream. One tap, it plays.

  • Zero servers Credentials live in your link. Nothing stored.
