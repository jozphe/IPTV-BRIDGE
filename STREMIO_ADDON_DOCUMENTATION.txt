IPTV Bridge
Stremio Addon Description and Validation Notes
================================================

IPTV Bridge is a serverless Stremio-compatible addon that connects a user's
Xtream Codes account or M3U/M3U8 playlist to Stremio and Nuvio. It organizes
IPTV live channels, VOD movies, and TV series into player-compatible catalogs
and returns the provider's direct playable stream URLs.


SUPPORTED CONTENT
------------------

1. Live TV channels

   Live IPTV channels are read from Xtream live streams or M3U channel entries.
   Channel names, group categories, TVG identifiers, logos, and direct live
   stream URLs are preserved where available.

2. VOD movies

   VOD means Video on Demand. VOD movies are read from Xtream VOD streams or
   detected from M3U movie groups and media extensions. Movies are organized in
   the IPTV Movies catalog and can also be resolved from normal Stremio/Nuvio
   movie pages by IMDB or TMDB identity.

3. TV series

   TV series are read from the Xtream series endpoint or detected from M3U
   series groups and season/episode patterns. Xtream series episodes are
   resolved through the provider's series information endpoint, including the
   requested season and episode.

4. VOD and series categories

   Xtream VOD categories, series categories, and live categories are requested
   separately. M3U group-title values are converted into addon categories.
   Users can select which categories and media types should be included in the
   generated addon configuration.


PLAYER INTEGRATION
------------------

The addon implements the Stremio Addon Protocol v3 resources:

    manifest
    catalog
    meta
    stream

The manifest advertises support for:

    tv
    movie
    series

The stream resource accepts:

    IPTV item IDs generated by this addon
    IMDB IDs beginning with "tt"
    TMDB IDs beginning with "tmdb:"

This allows IPTV Bridge to contribute streams when a movie or series is opened
from Stremio or Nuvio's normal Popular, Featured, Search, or catalog results.


METADATA AND TITLE RESOLUTION
-----------------------------

TMDB is used for optional metadata enrichment, including:

    posters
    backdrops
    descriptions
    release years
    ratings
    genres
    series episode presentation

The provider remains the source of truth for playable IPTV content. TMDB does
not provide or proxy the video stream.

Before matching, IPTV titles are normalized by removing provider metadata such
as:

    language and country prefixes
    quality labels such as FHD, 1080p, UHD, and 4K
    codec and release labels
    years used as release metadata
    M3U punctuation and formatting differences

Exact normalized title identity is preferred. Multiple streams with the same
title identity, such as FHD and 4K variants, are preserved. Different titles,
sequels, and similarly named movies are rejected where the identity does not
match.


SMALL ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
---------------------------

The addon runs as a stateless Vercel serverless application:

    Stremio/Nuvio
          |
          v
    Vercel serverless route
          |
          +--> Xtream Codes API or M3U parser
          |
          +--> title normalization and exact matching
          |
          +--> optional TMDB metadata resolution
          |
          v
    direct IPTV stream URL returned to the player

The addon does not proxy, relay, download, cache, or transcode video. After a
stream response is returned, Stremio or Nuvio connects directly to the IPTV
provider URL.

Configuration is encoded into the addon manifest URL. No IPTV database is
required for normal operation. Warm serverless instances use bounded in-memory
caches for provider lists, categories, episodes, and TMDB lookups.


VALIDATION SUMMARY
------------------

The addon can be validated against the following requirements:

    [PASS] Stremio manifest resource is available
    [PASS] Nuvio-compatible Stremio protocol resources are available
    [PASS] Live TV channel catalog is supported
    [PASS] VOD movie catalog is supported
    [PASS] TV series catalog is supported
    [PASS] Xtream VOD categories are supported
    [PASS] Xtream series categories are supported
    [PASS] M3U group categories are supported
    [PASS] IMDB movie and series stream resolution is supported
    [PASS] TMDB movie and series stream resolution is supported
    [PASS] Exact title matching is used for provider streams
    [PASS] Multiple quality variants can be returned for one exact title
    [PASS] Direct IPTV playback URLs are returned without media proxying
    [PASS] TMDB metadata can enrich the player presentation


IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------

The addon can only return a stream when the connected IPTV provider contains a
matching movie, VOD item, channel, series, or requested episode. TMDB metadata
availability does not guarantee that the user's IPTV subscription contains the
corresponding playable content.

Playback buffering is normally controlled by the IPTV provider, the provider's
stream server, the user's network, ISP routing, VPN, codec compatibility, or
the Stremio/Nuvio player. IPTV Bridge only resolves and returns the direct
provider stream URL.

Users must connect only IPTV sources they are authorized to use.
